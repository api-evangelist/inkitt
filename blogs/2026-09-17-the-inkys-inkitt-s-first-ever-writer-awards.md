Meet The Inkys We are excited to announce Inkitt’s first annual writing awards, celebrating the stories and storytellers that made 2026 a year for the books. Our inaugural 2026 awards will recognize standout stories across romance, romantasy, sci-fi, action, fan fiction, AI-assisted storytelling, and international fiction. Whether you published your first story this year or gave readers their newest obsession, this is your chance to put your work in the running to win an Inky.
